The Rules

Understand these before the sprint begins — no exceptions on blitz day.

1
Net New Accounts Only
Accounts must have zero prior SAP engagement. No existing customers, no active opportunities, no install base. If SAP is already in conversation with the account, it does not count.
2
Pre-Approved List Required
Only accounts from your SAP-approved list submitted before July 14th count toward the leaderboard. Off-list accounts will be disqualified regardless of lead quality.
3
Account Exclusivity — First Submission Wins
Account lists are confidential between each partner and SAP only. In the event of overlap, the partner who submitted first retains the account. Displaced accounts must be replaced before July 18th.
4
Leaderboard Shows Numbers Only
Live standings track contacts made, conversations held, and leads qualified. No account names are ever visible to competing partners at any stage.
5
4:45 PM Hard Submission Deadline
All leads must be submitted via this site before 4:45 PM on July 21st. Late submissions will not be counted toward the leaderboard.

What Counts as a Qualified Lead?

All three criteria must be met — no exceptions.

C
Executive Contact Reached
C-suite, VP, or Director level — a decision-maker or strong influencer in the buying process.
8C
At Least One 8C Criterion Confirmed
Compelling Event, Funding, Customer Stakeholder Strategy, Customer Challenges, Business Value Drivers, Solution & Differentiators, Competitors, or Ecosystem — at least one acknowledged on the call.
N
Follow-up Action Agreed
A concrete next step confirmed: discovery call booked, demo scheduled, or proposal requested.

The 8 Deal Qualification Criteria

1
Compelling Event
Why is the customer looking for a new solution, and why are they doing that right now?
2
Funding
Has funding been secured? Where does the money come from, and what are the investment constraints?
3
Customer Stakeholder Strategy
Who is impacted and who makes or influences decisions?
4
Customer Challenges
What are they struggling with?
5
Customer Business Value Drivers
How does each of the stakeholders measure success?
6
Solution & Differentiators
Why is SAP the best choice for this particular customer project and why this product from the SAP portfolio?
7
Competitors
Who are we competing against? How strong are we in comparison? Which other projects are we competing with for the budget?
8
Ecosystem
Which partners are already involved? Which partners should be?
⚠️
Leads submitted without meeting all three qualification criteria will be reviewed by SAP Egypt and will not count toward the leaderboard if they fail the standard.
